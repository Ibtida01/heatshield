    How HeatShield works

    1. A standard Bluetooth heart-rate band sends one reading per minute to the phone.
    2. The phone estimates core body temperature from the heart-rate trend with a published Kalman filter (Buller et al., 2013).
    3. A safety margin from conformal prediction is added, and alerts use the top of that margin so a hot worker is not missed.
    4. Site weather is turned into WBGT heat stress with the Liljegren model, using Open-Meteo data.
    5. The phone projects the next hour two ways, keep working or rest now, and counts the minutes until danger.
    6. Each shift adds to a weekly heat-load record; repeated hard days trigger a kidney-check prompt.

    Privacy

    • All estimates run on the worker's phone. Nothing leaves it unless a crew is linked.
    • Supervisors see the sign, the countdown and rest status only.
    • No use for discipline, hiring or insurance decisions.
    • Workers can stop monitoring at any time and delete their data from the phone.

    Limits

    • HeatShield is not a medical device. It does not diagnose heat illness or kidney disease.
    • The heart-rate estimator was developed on soldiers and athletes; it must be validated in outdoor workers.
    • Fever, medicines such as beta-blockers, dehydration and anxiety change heart rate and can mislead the estimate.
    • Anyone who feels dizzy, confused, sick or stops sweating needs first aid now, whatever the app shows.

    Sources

    • Buller MJ et al. Estimation of human core temperature from sequential heart rate observations. Physiol Meas 2013.
    • Liljegren JC et al. Modeling the wet bulb globe temperature using standard meteorological measurements. J Occup Environ Hyg 2008.
    • Moran DS et al. A physiological strain index to evaluate heat stress. Am J Physiol 1998.
    • Weather: Open-Meteo forecast and historical APIs (CC BY 4.0).

    Before you start

    • HeatShield reads your heart rate from a band and the weather for your site.
    • Your body heat estimate and heart rate stay on this phone.
    • Your supervisor sees only your sign colour, the minutes until danger and whether you are resting.
    • This is not a medical device. If you feel dizzy, sick or confused, stop and get help.
    • You can stop monitoring and delete your data at any time.
