# Pitch deck outline (10 slides, 5 minutes at GITEX)

1. **Title.** HeatShield: heat-strain early warning for outdoor workers. One line: "Rest before it's too late." Team names.
2. **The problem, in one picture.** Your site photo plus three facts with sources: Gulf summer temperatures near 50 °C; nephrologists in Dhaka and Kathmandu seeing returnees with kidney disease; fixed midday rules do not cover every risky hour.
3. **Who we serve.** A worker (low literacy, Bengali speaker, new arrival), a supervisor, a clinic. One sentence each on what they need.
4. **The product.** Three phone screenshots: green square, amber triangle with countdown, red stop sign. "Danger in 18 minutes."
5. **How it works.** The six-step pipeline from the About screen as a simple diagram: band → estimate → safety margin → WBGT → forecast → alert and weekly ledger.
6. **Evidence.** Bland–Altman plot and the sensitivity table (estimate vs upper bound) from `research/results/`. WBGT checked against the reference within 0.01 °C.
7. **Responsible AI.** On-device; supervisors see tiers only; rules decide alerts; model card; no use for discipline; not a medical device.
8. **Why it scales.** Standard bands and phones, a free static app, offline-first, six languages; it builds on the UAE midday break by making protection risk-based between the fixed hours.
9. **Business and pilot.** Per-worker subscription for contractors; pilot of 30–50 workers for 8 weeks in summer 2027; outcomes measured; research study in Dhaka with **FILL clinical partner**.
10. **Ask.** A pilot site, clinical validation support from the Future Health ecosystem, and introductions to contractors' HSE teams.

Design: reuse the app's signs, colours and Barlow typeface for the deck so the product and pitch look like one system.
